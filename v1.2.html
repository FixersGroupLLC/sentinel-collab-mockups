Every number is a true action

Sentinel Foundation has grown from a small, specialized team into an established operational partner in counter-trafficking, crisis response and intelligence support.

Their intelligence, field and technical teams have supported rescues, assisted in trafficker arrests, trained law enforcement and partners, and in 2025 alone worked 680+ cases across 9 countries.

Sentinel works through formal agreements and direct operational relationships with national and international law enforcement and partner organizations.

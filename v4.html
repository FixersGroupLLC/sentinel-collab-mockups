Mission brief

Until every child is safe.

Sentinel Foundation helps protect vulnerable children through counter-trafficking support, crisis response, and advanced training for those on the front lines.

Its teams work alongside law enforcement and trusted partners to identify victims, develop actionable intelligence, and support operations designed to disrupt the individuals and networks responsible for child exploitation.

Savage Tacticians is partnering with Sentinel to help fund that work. Every collab shirt sold sends a portion of the proceeds to Sentinel Foundation.

For every collab shirt sold, we donate a portion of the proceeds to help fuel Sentinel Foundation's mission.

Who is Sentinel Foundation?

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit that helps protect vulnerable children through counter-trafficking support, crisis response, and advanced training for those on the front lines.

Where do the numbers come from?

They come from Sentinel Foundation's 2025 Annual Report, published on their website. All figures are for 2025 except the aftercare figure, which is cumulative.

● File 04 / Debrief

Questions, answered

The short version, before you gear up.

What happens when I buy one?

You get the shirt, and a portion of the proceeds goes to Sentinel Foundation to help fuel their mission.

Who is Sentinel Foundation, in plain terms?

A counter-trafficking group that started small. Today they help with rescues, help police arrest traffickers, train officers, and step in on crisis response.

Do they work with law enforcement?

Yes. They work with police and trusted partner groups, at home and abroad.

Where do the numbers on this page come from?

Sentinel Foundation's own 2025 Annual Report, posted on foundationsentinel.org. Each figure on this page is labeled 2025 or cumulative, and we did not add any numbers of our own.

What am I getting?

A graphic tee, $34.99, in Black or OD Green, sizes S to 3XL, with the collab art shown on this page.

Where do I buy it?

On sav-tac.com. The buttons on this page take you to the product page, where you pick your color and size.
